Members

Encore Wales members use Chorago for everything to do with our shows: scripts, rehearsal notes, choreography videos and announcements from the production team.

Already have an account?

Sign in to Chorago

New member?

Ask the production team for a join link at rehearsal, or email us at info@encorewales.co.uk. Once you've signed up, the team will approve your account before you can see anything. That keeps everything, including photos of our younger members, private to the group.

Get announcements on your phone

Open chorago.com on your phone and add it to your home screen. You can then turn on notifications so you never miss a rehearsal change.
